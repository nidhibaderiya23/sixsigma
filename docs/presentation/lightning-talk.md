# Format 4: 60-second lightning talk

For the closing ceremony, if asked. One speaker, no slides (or deck 1's cover on screen).

> Ten ambulance calls average 9.2 minutes, right at the 9-minute target. But four of them were
> late, and one patient waited 20 minutes. That is what a monthly average hides.
>
> We built ERGent for Ithaca's 911 center. The dispatcher gets live clocks and a map of Ithaca that
> turns amber and red while a call is still running. The supervisor gets a daily control chart for
> every zone, a split of every delay into the dispatcher, the crew and the drive, and a capability
> score in plain words.
>
> On our test data, ERGent found that Zone D's night crews were slow to get out the door, suggested
> a fix, and two weeks later showed on-time calls going from 78% to 97%, with a t-test to prove it
> wasn't chance.
>
> Every zone, every stage, every day. We're Team ERG. Thank you.

About 150 words: about 60 seconds at a calm pace.
