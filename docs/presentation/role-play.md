# Format 3: live role-play (about 6 minutes, no slides)

Three people, one laptop on the projector (or two laptops, one per role). The story is the same as
deck 1, told through the app itself.

| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| Dhruv | **The dispatcher** on the night shift, at the live board |
| Zeba | **The supervisor**, at the start of the shift |
| Nidhi | **The narrator**: asks the questions the audience would ask |

## Script

**Nidhi (0:00):** "Ithaca's 911 center gets a report every month. Last month's average response was
5.6 minutes, comfortably under the 9-minute target. Everyone's happy. Let's see one night."

**Dhruv (0:20):** opens `/?role=dispatcher`, sets **Speed 120x**, **Start 22:00**, clicks
**Simulate a shift**. "I'm dispatching. Calls come in here, I send an ambulance, and every stage has
a clock. Watch this one: it's amber, 80% of its target. Now it's red." Points at the Ithaca map:
"Each zone takes the colour of its worst open call."

**Nidhi (1:30):** "So the dispatcher sees it in the moment. But who notices that it keeps happening
in the same place?"

**Zeba (1:45):** opens `/?role=supervisor`, sets **As of 2026-09-28**. "I do. Every morning. The city
is at 93% on time, but Zone D is amber." Hovers Zone D: "Its 90th percentile is 10.2 minutes, and
the main delay is crew turnout." Scrolls to Delay by stage: "92% of the minutes over target are the
crew getting out the door, not the drive."

**Nidhi (2:45):** "Is that real, or bad luck?"

**Zeba (3:00):** points at Zone comparison: "An ANOVA says the gap is very unlikely to be chance." At
Capability: "Only about 89 in 100 calls arrive in time." Opens **Actions**: "ERGent suggests a
review of crew readiness at Zone D's station, especially the night shift, and estimates 78% to 97%
on time if turnout were typical. I log it."

**Nidhi (4:00):** "Two weeks later?"

**Zeba (4:10):** sets **As of 2026-10-12**: "Zone D is green. The before-and-after check says
Improved: 78% to 97% on time, and Welch's test says it is not chance. And look: Zone C just turned
amber. Road works at rush hour. The next problem, found on day one."

**Dhruv (5:00):** back on the board, opens **History**: "And I get tips for right now: dispatch now,
rebalance, route around the road works."

**Nidhi (5:30):** "ERGent: every zone, every stage, every day, so no patient hides inside an
average. Questions?"
